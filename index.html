Patterns

Save everything you hear: every control, plus the exact phrase (notes, lengths, rests, inflections) and the vowel sequence. Click a saved pattern to bring it back; it starts from a clean statement of the phrase.

Export

The last minute of what actually played is kept in memory while Choir runs, randomness and all. Press Capture last minute, tap the timeline to select a stretch, preview it, then save it as a WAV (exactly what you heard, reverb included) or a MIDI file (one track per voice, for a DAW). Nothing is uploaded.
